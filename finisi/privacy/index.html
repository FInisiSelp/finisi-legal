Finisi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Finisi ("Finisi", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Finisi app for iPhone (bundle ID com.finisi.rdfux). Finisi is a wear log for gel manicures: you record each set's base coat, color gel, top coat, lamp and prep, add photos and an optional voice note, mark the set's condition day by day, and see which formulas last longest.

If you have questions, contact us at FInisimail@gmail.com.

1. Information you provide

Finisi only works with information you choose to add:

  • Nail set records — set date, products used for each layer, prep and technique tags, daily status marks (Intact, Lifting, Chipping, Popped off), removal reason and notes (up to 140 characters).
  • Photos — photos of your finished set or of a lifted edge, taken with the camera or picked from your photo library (up to 3 per set).
  • Voice notes — optional recordings of up to 30 seconds attached to a set.
  • Custom products — brand, product name, layer and optional details (viscosity class, volume, wavelength, cure time) for products you add to your library.
  • Profile preferences — dominant hand, nail plate type and wear target, chosen during setup or in Settings.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or location, and Finisi does not have a sign-up form.

2. How your information is stored

Your set records, photos, voice notes, custom products and preferences are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone. When you tap Start my log, Finisi creates a random identifier so the app can recognise your session; it is not linked to your identity.

Finisi does not upload your records, photos or voice notes to our systems, and we cannot see them. Your iPhone's own iCloud or device backup settings may include app data in your personal backups, as controlled by Apple and by you.

3. How we use information

Information you add is used only to provide the app's features:

  • showing your current set, timeline and set details;
  • calculating wear days, averages, best results and the comparisons shown in Insights;
  • generating comparison cards, wear report PDFs and JSON exports when you ask for them;
  • recognising text on a product label when you use Scan Bottle Label (see section 6).

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell personal data.

4. Facial data

4.1 What is collected

Finisi does not collect facial data. It does not use Face ID data, the TrueDepth camera, face detection, face recognition, facial geometry or any biometric faceprint. Photos in Finisi are intended to show nails and hands. A photo you take or pick could incidentally include part of a face; Finisi stores such a photo only as an ordinary image file and does not analyse faces in it.

4.2 How it is used

Photos are used only to display your nail set inside the app (set cards, set detail, timeline and the optional overlay that helps you match your last set's angle) and, if you choose, to create a comparison card. No facial information is extracted, measured or derived from any photo.

4.3 Sharing and disclosure

Photos are not shared with us or with any third party. They are stored in Finisi's storage on your iPhone. A photo leaves the app only if you export it yourself, for example by saving a comparison card to Photos or sharing it through the iOS share sheet.

4.4 Retention

Photos are kept until you delete them: remove a photo from a set, delete the set, or use Settings → Delete All Data, which permanently removes all photos stored by Finisi. Deleting the app also removes them from your iPhone.

4.5 This chapter

This chapter (sections 4.1–4.5) is Finisi's complete explanation of what facial data it collects, how it is used, whether it is disclosed or shared, where it is stored and how long it is retained.

5. Permissions

Finisi asks for each permission through the standard iOS dialog. You can change any of them at any time in iOS Settings.

Permission When it is requested Why
Camera When you open the capture guide To photograph your nail set or a lifted edge, and to photograph a product label for scanning
Photo Library When you choose a photo from your library To pick a photo for a set or a product label
Photo Library (add only) When you first save a comparison card To save the card to your Photos
Microphone When you first record a voice note To record a short voice note about a set
Notifications The first time you reach the Current screen Finisi sends notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings

If you decline a permission, the related feature is unavailable, and the rest of the app keeps working.

6. Label scanning

Scan Bottle Label uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs on your iPhone, to read text from a photo of a product label and pre-fill the brand and product name fields. The photo and the recognised text are not sent to us or to any third-party service. Finisi does not use any third-party AI service.

7. In-app purchases

Finisi offers Lab Tokens as consumable in-app purchases (seven packs, from $0.99 to $99.99). Tokens can be spent on watermark-free comparison cards, wear report PDFs and extra custom product slots. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details or your Apple ID. Your token balance and spending history are stored in the app on your iPhone.

8. Exports

When you export a comparison card, a wear report PDF or your data as JSON, Finisi creates the file and hands it to the destination you choose (Photos, Files, Mail, Messages or another app). Once exported, the file is handled by that destination and is outside Finisi's control.

9. Contact us

If you tap Contact us in Settings, Finisi opens an email draft addressed to FInisimail@gmail.com in your Mail app. We receive only what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you.

10. Deleting your data

  • Delete a set — removes that set's record, photos and voice note.
  • End Session (Settings) — closes your session and returns you to the start screen. Your records are kept for the next time you tap Start my log.
  • Delete All Data (Settings) — after a confirmation, permanently deletes every set, photo, voice note, custom product, preference, token balance and session identifier stored by Finisi, and returns you to the start screen. Built-in content (the Finisi Guide example set and the built-in product library) remains, as on a fresh install. This cannot be undone.

11. Third parties

Finisi contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or third-party tracking. The only outside service involved is Apple, for App Store purchases and, if you allow it, notifications.

12. Children's privacy

Finisi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information through Contact us, email us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Because your records stay in the app on your iPhone, you can view, export (Settings → Export data (JSON)) and delete them at any time without contacting us. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of Finisi after an update means you accept the revised policy.
