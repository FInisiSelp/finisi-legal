1. The service

Finisi is a wear log for gel manicures. It lets you:

  • record each nail set's base coat, color gels, top coat, lamp, prep and techniques, with photos and an optional voice note;
  • mark each day's condition with one tap and record when and why a set was removed;
  • review your sets in a timeline and see wear-day comparisons in Insights;
  • keep a product library (built-in products plus products you add, including by scanning a label);
  • export comparison cards, wear report PDFs and your data as JSON.

Finisi shows statistics calculated from your own records. It does not give professional, medical, cosmetic or chemical-safety advice, does not evaluate products for safety and does not recommend products. Results depend on your own entries and are for your personal reference.

2. Getting started and your session

  • Start my log — opens Finisi without entering any personal details. The app creates a random identifier for your session.
  • End Session (Settings) — closes your session and returns you to the start screen. Your records are kept for the next time you tap Start my log.
  • Delete All Data (Settings) — permanently deletes all records, photos, voice notes, custom products, preferences and token balance stored by Finisi. This cannot be undone, and unused Lab Tokens are lost.
